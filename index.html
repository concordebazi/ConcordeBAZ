About Me

GIS & Geomatics Engineer | Map Engineer

I am a GIS and Geomatics Engineer with a Master's degree in Geomatics from Lund University, Sweden, specializing in geographic information systems, cartography, spatial data management, and marine geospatial applications.

My professional experience combines GIS engineering, map production, hydrographic data processing, and geospatial analysis across public and private sectors.

I have worked with geographic databases, digital mapping, hydrographic survey data, bathymetry, marine cartography, and spatial information solutions.

My goal is to transform complex geospatial data into accurate, reliable, and meaningful information that supports decision-making.
