Swedish Maritime Administration

Map Engineer

Supporting safe navigation through hydrographic data management, GIS, cartography and digital nautical chart production.

Project Overview

As a Map Engineer at the Swedish Maritime Administration (Sjöfartsverket), I worked with hydrographic and geospatial information to support safe navigation and the production of high-quality nautical products.

My responsibilities included processing hydrographic survey data, maintaining spatial databases, performing quality assurance and contributing to the production and updating of digital nautical charts and other marine geospatial products.

This role combined GIS, cartography, hydrography and data management, ensuring that geographic information met national standards and supported maritime navigation.

My Responsibilities

🗺 Digital Cartography

Prepared, updated and maintained digital nautical charts and cartographic products.

🌊 Hydrographic Data

Processed hydrographic survey information and maintained high data quality standards.

🗄 Spatial Databases

Maintained hydrographic and geographic databases used for marine navigation.

📍 GIS Analysis

Performed GIS analysis and spatial data management to support mapping activities.

✅ Quality Assurance

Verified geospatial information before publication and distribution.

🤝 Collaboration

Worked with hydrographers, surveyors and GIS specialists to produce reliable mapping products.
