Swedish Maritime Administration

Map Engineer

Supporting safe navigation through hydrographic data management, GIS, cartography and digital nautical chart production.

Project Overview

As a Map Engineer at the Swedish Maritime Administration (Sjöfartsverket), I worked with hydrographic and geospatial information to support safe navigation and the production of high-quality nautical products.

My responsibilities included processing hydrographic survey data, maintaining spatial databases, performing quality assurance and contributing to the production and updating of digital nautical charts and other marine geospatial products.

This role combined GIS, cartography, hydrography and data management, ensuring that geographic information met national standards and supported maritime navigation.

Core Responsibilities

🗺 Digital Cartography

Prepared and maintained digital nautical charts and cartographic products used to support safe maritime navigation.

🌊 Hydrographic Data Management

Processed, validated and maintained hydrographic survey information for national marine datasets.

🗄 Geospatial Database Maintenance

Updated and maintained hydrographic and geographic databases, ensuring data accuracy and consistency.

📍 GIS Analysis

Performed GIS analysis and spatial data management to support mapping, planning and decision-making.

✅ Quality Assurance

Reviewed geospatial datasets and verified compliance with organisational standards before publication.

🤝 Team Collaboration

Worked closely with hydrographers, surveyors, GIS specialists and cartographers to deliver high-quality mapping products.
